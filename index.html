      Method & caveats

      FOIA 20394 arrived as a single 514 MB PDF portfolio of 1,167 emails. Each cited email was extracted byte-for-byte from that portfolio as its own PDF. SHA-256 hashes for every hosted file, and for the original portfolio, are in requests/bluffton/SHA256SUMS. Document IDs E#### are the item numbers inside the portfolio. Three emails appear only as redacted transcriptions: one contained a plaintext password for the Town's camera system, and two name private individuals who own camera sites. Text was extracted with PyMuPDF and pdftotext. Page numbers in citations are PDF page numbers.

      Officers, Town staff and Flock employees are named because they're acting in their public or contractual roles. Private residents and landowners are not named.

      Findings describe what the documents say and don't say. Something absent from this release isn't proof it doesn't exist, only that it wasn't produced. Quotes are non-binding proposals unless matched by a signed order.
