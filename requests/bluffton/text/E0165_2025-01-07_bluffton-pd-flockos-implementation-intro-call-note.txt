Map
Attachments
1/7 - Your agency can add map attachments at any time (no need to send files to Flock); we
support PDFs, Word docs, images (jpg, png), or website links as locations that show as pins
on the map. Examples include floor plans, links directly to a VMS, a link to instructions for a
special event in the area, primary contacts for a school, etc. This link has the step-by-step
instructions to add attachments: https://help.flocksafety.com/s/article/Map-Attachments.
 
Note: A Flock Admin user will need to add the Floor Plans permission for other Roles

Planned
ESRI Layers
1/7 - We can bring in your ESRI layers to give you more detail on your FlockOS map. We
typically upload beats, city limits, and parcel layers for addresses, though we can upload
more (ex: hunting zones, horse trails, etc). Two ways we can load your ESRI layers: you can
send us a link to a publicly hosted ESRI feature server, or you can send us zipped shape files
that we can host for you. The Chief will identify the resource to send the layers (this can
often be someone from the GIS team)
