TOWN OF BLUFFTON LAW ENFORCEMENT CITIZENS ADVISORY COMMITTEE MEETING Thursday, February 8, 2024
Members present: Michael Frazier, George Baily, Mayra Rivera-Vazquez, Tabor Vaux
Members Not Present: Bill O’Toole, Jennifer Morrow, Reggie Howard
Staff Present: Chief Joe Babkiewicz, Sergeant M. Ferrelli
Michael Frazier called the meeting to order at 18:00 hrs. at the Henry “Emmett” McCracken Council Chambers located at 20 Bridge Street, followed by the Pledge of Allegiance. 
Public Comment:Betty Black (38 Wiregrass Way, Bluffton, SC 29910). Ms. Black stated that this was her first time attending and wanted to observe the goings on at the LECAC meetings. She also voiced her praises for the FLOCK cameras that are currently being used in her neighborhood (Pine Ridge). 
Old Business:
- Mike Frazier made a motion to approve the prior meeting minutes, was seconded by Myra Rivera-Vazquez
- Mike Frazier made a motion to approve the agenda for this meeting, was seconded by George Baily.
- Mike Frazier made a motion to approve the 2024 LECAC meeting schedule, was seconded by Myra Rivera-Vazquez (Tabor Vaux also moved to approve with any future schedule modifications final approval by chairman Jennifer Morrow as see fit).
New Business:
- Chief Babkiewicz presented 3 newly revised Bluffton Police Department Policies to the committee for their review, and discussed the changes made to each one. 
-BPD Pursuit Policy- George Baily motion to approve policy changes, seconded by Mike Frazier. 
-BPD Response to Resistance Policy
- Mike Frazier motion to approve policy changes, seconded by George Baily.
-BPD Discharging of Firearm Policy- George Baily motion to approve policy changes, seconded by Mike Frazier. 
Discussion:
-Chief Babkiewicz discussed the department stats in comparison to surrounding cities and towns of similar size within the state. 
-Chief Babkiewicz also discussed the department’s desire to obtain FLOCK camera systems, to be implemented and used throughout the town in hopes of increasing the probability of solving various crimes. 
A motion to adjourn was made at 1900 hrs. by Mike Frazier, and all members seconded the motion. 
The next scheduled LECAC meeting is slated for March 14th, 2024, at 1800hrs. 
Sergeant Matt Ferrelli Secretary
